What PDF Reader installs

- The app, in your user folder (no administrator rights needed).
- A Start menu shortcut, and a desktop shortcut if you choose one.
- The natural voices (about 354 MB), downloaded now if you choose, or the first time the app starts. Each file is checked against its published fingerprint (SHA-256) before it is used.

Your privacy

- Everything runs on your own computer. Your documents never leave your PC.
- No account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing runs in the background.
- The app only goes online to download the voices, and when you open a web link.

Removing it

- Use Settings > Apps > Installed apps > PDF Reader > Uninstall. You'll be asked whether to also remove the downloaded voices and your settings.
